# POS System - Resume Features

## Core Features

• **Point of Sale (POS) Interface** - Real-time sales processing with product search, cart management, and receipt generation

• **Multi-Store Management** - Support for multiple store locations with inter-store stock transfers and store-specific data isolation

• **Inventory Management** - Stock tracking, low stock alerts, stock adjustments, and automated stock level monitoring

• **Product Management** - Complete product catalog with SKU, barcode, categories, units, wholesale/retail pricing, and tax rates

• **Sales & Purchase Management** - Comprehensive sales tracking, purchase order management, and transaction history

• **Returns Processing** - Sale and purchase return management with full audit trail

• **Customer & Supplier Management** - Complete CRM with customer credit accounts, supplier database, and relationship tracking

• **Credit Management** - Customer credit accounts with balance tracking, payment history, and debt management

• **Quotation System** - Professional quote generation with tax calculations and discount application

• **Expense Tracking** - Business expense management and categorization

• **Payment Processing** - Multiple payment methods (Cash, Mobile Money, Bank Transfer, Credit, Cheque) with split payment support

• **Tax & Discount Management** - Automatic tax calculation per product and order-level discount application

• **Reports & Analytics** - Real-time dashboard with revenue metrics, sales statistics, inventory insights, and business KPIs

• **User Management & Permissions** - Role-based access control (Admin, Manager, Cashier) with granular feature permissions

• **Mobile Application** - React Native mobile app for on-the-go inventory monitoring, sales tracking, and dashboard access

• **Authentication & Security** - Secure authentication with Supabase, row-level security policies, and data access controls

• **Settings & Configuration** - Company settings, multi-currency support, theme customization (dark/light mode), and system preferences

## Technical Highlights

• Built with **Next.js 16**, **React 19**, **TypeScript**, and **Supabase** (PostgreSQL)
• **Responsive Design** with modern UI components (Radix UI, Tailwind CSS)
• **Mobile App** built with **React Native** and **Expo**
• **Real-time Data** synchronization across web and mobile platforms
• **Database Migrations** and schema management with SQL scripts
• **Server-Side Rendering** for optimal performance and SEO
